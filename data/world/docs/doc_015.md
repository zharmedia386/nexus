# 2021 Corporate Roundup: Key Partnership and Investment Milestones

**Jakarta, 2021** – This news roundup highlights significant strategic developments and historical milestones across several Indonesian corporate entities, underscoring the continued growth and collaboration within the local business landscape.

**PT Sinar Semesta Capital** and **PT Bintang Harmoni**
In a notable move for early-stage venture capital, **PT Sinar Semesta Capital** invested in **PT Bintang Harmoni** during the Seed round. This investment, valued at IDR 347 billion, was completed in 2017, marking a substantial commitment to the company's initial growth phase.

**PT Rimba Harmoni** and **PT Delta Kreasi Tbk**
Expanding its market reach, **PT Rimba Harmoni** has maintained a distribution partnership with **PT Delta Kreasi Tbk** since 2018. This ongoing collaboration has been instrumental in enhancing product availability and market penetration for both parties.

**Tirta Kencana** and **Arta Mulia**
Demonstrating long-term stability in their supply chain, **Tirta Kencana** has held a supply partnership with **Arta Mulia** since 2012. This enduring relationship highlights the consistent operational synergy between the two entities over the past decade.
