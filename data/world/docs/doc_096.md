# Grup Mulia Lancar: Group Structure Overview

This note provides an overview of the corporate structure within the Grup Mulia Lancar ecosystem. Based on our observations, the group comprises several distinct entities that operate under the unified banner of the Grup Mulia Lancar.

The membership of the Grup Mulia Lancar includes the following entities:
*   PT Mulia Nirwana is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
*   Citra Semesta is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
*   PT Jaya Agung is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
*   Sinar Cemerlang is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
*   PT Lintas Raya is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
*   PT Mulia Agung is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
*   PT Tunas Makmur is a member of the Grup Mulia Lancar.

We note that the group structure is characterized by a diverse set of constituent companies, ranging from entities with the "PT" designation to those without, all maintaining their status as members of the Grup Mulia Lancar.
