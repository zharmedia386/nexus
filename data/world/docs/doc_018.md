# 2023 Corporate Roundup: Key Partnerships and Investment Milestones

**Jakarta, 2023** – In a year marked by strategic expansion and capital deployment, several prominent Indonesian corporate entities have solidified their market positions through significant partnerships and investments. Below is a roundup of key corporate developments recorded in 2023.

**PT Jaya Agung** continues to leverage its long-standing commercial relationships to drive operational efficiency. The company has maintained a supply partnership with Mulia Abadi since 2016, a collaboration that has remained a cornerstone of their respective supply chains.

In the venture capital sector, **Indah Lestari Capital** demonstrated its commitment to high-growth opportunities by investing in PT Rimba Raya in the Seri C round. This investment, valued at IDR 158 billion, was executed in 2019 and has since supported PT Rimba Raya’s scaling efforts.

Looking to the present, **Citra Lancar** has expanded its strategic footprint by establishing a joint venture with Prima Makmur since 2023. This new alliance reflects a proactive approach to market development and shared resource utilization in the current fiscal year.
