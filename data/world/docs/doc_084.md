# Corporate Roundup: Buana Persada and PT Bintang Kencana Leadership and Ownership Details

**JAKARTA** – A review of corporate governance and ownership structures at two Indonesian entities, Buana Persada and PT Bintang Kencana, highlights long-standing leadership tenures and recent shifts in major shareholder stakes.

At Buana Persada, the company’s history traces back to its founding by Calista Astuti in 1984. Astuti has maintained a significant influence on the company, holding a 39% stake in Buana Persada since 2012. The current executive and supervisory board reflects a stable leadership structure. Nadia Hariyah has served as Direktur Utama at Buana Persada since 2011, while Rama Marpaung has held the position of Direktur since 2012. On the supervisory side, Rina Pratiwi has served as Komisaris Utama at Buana Persada since 2020.

In a separate development, PT Bintang Kencana, which was founded by Lantar Natsir in 1977, has seen a change in its controlling shareholder. Lantar Natsir held a 56% stake in PT Bintang Kencana from 2006 until 2021. Following this period, Malik Dongoran has held a 56% stake in PT Bintang Kencana since 2021. The company’s management team has remained consistent, with Kartika Purnawati serving as Direktur Utama at PT Bintang Kencana since 2014 and Gawati Novitasari serving as Direktur since 2015. Lintang Widiastuti has served as Komisaris Utama at PT Bintang Kencana since 2009.
