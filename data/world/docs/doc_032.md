# Pelita Gemilang Capital: Company Profile, Board Composition and Shareholder Structure

## Company Profile

Pelita Gemilang Capital operates as a diversified investment holding company with a portfolio of strategic subsidiaries across various sectors. The group's corporate structure is anchored by four key subsidiaries, each of which has been under the ownership of Pelita Gemilang Capital for a significant period.

PT Nusantara Teguh has been a subsidiary of Pelita Gemilang Capital since 2006. Similarly, PT Citra Mulia has served as a subsidiary of Pelita Gemilang Capital since 2010. PT Dharma Agung Tbk has been a subsidiary of Pelita Gemilang Capital since 2012, while Jaya Sentosa has been a subsidiary of Pelita Gemilang Capital since 2019.

## Shareholder Structure

The shareholder composition of the group's subsidiaries reflects long-term strategic holdings by Pelita Gemilang Capital, alongside specific minority stakes held by other entities.

**PT Dharma Agung Tbk**
Pelita Gemilang Capital has held a 68% stake in PT Dharma Agung Tbk since 2018. Additionally, Nusantara Kreasi Sejahtera has held a 9% stake in PT Dharma Agung Tbk since 2021.

**Jaya Sentosa**
Pelita Gemilang Capital has held a 70% stake in Jaya Sentosa since 2021.

**PT Nusantara Teguh**
Pelita Gemilang Capital has held a 78% stake in PT Nusantara Teguh since 2014.

**PT Citra Mulia**
Pelita Gemilang Capital has held a 67% stake in PT Citra Mulia since 2007.

## Board Composition

The leadership teams of the group's subsidiaries are composed of experienced directors and commissioners who have served in their respective roles for varying durations.

**PT Dharma Agung Tbk**
The board of PT Dharma Agung Tbk includes Ophelia Jailani, who has served as Direktur Utama since 2011. Lantar Natsir has served as Direktur Keuangan since 2013. The supervisory board comprises Luwes Jailani, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2020, along with Kanda Permadi and Hilda Halimah, both of whom have served as Komisaris Independen since 2013.

**Jaya Sentosa**
Vinsen Jailani has served as Direktur Utama at Jaya Sentosa since 2017. Jaiman Mahendra has served as Direktur at Jaya Sentosa since 2018. Heru Hidayat has served as Komisaris Utama at Jaya Sentosa since 2018.

**PT Nusantara Teguh**
Maryadi Thamrin has served as Direktur Utama at PT Nusantara Teguh since 2016. Ratna Haryanti has served as Direktur at PT Nusantara Teguh since 2015. Kurnia Mansur has served as Komisaris Utama at PT Nusantara Teguh since 2016.

**PT Citra Mulia**
Galang Sitompul has served as Direktur Utama at PT Citra Mulia since 2006. Eja Zulkarnain has served as Direktur at PT Citra Mulia since 2014. Luwes Jailani has served as Komisaris Utama at PT Citra Mulia since 2009.
