# Group Structure: Bumi Sentosa Group

The Bumi Sentosa Group comprises a diversified portfolio of entities. PT Pelita Perkasa Tbk is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group. PT Bintang Teguh is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group. PT Mega Persada Tbk is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group. WTOY is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group. Gemilang Sakti is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group. Pelita Bahari is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group. PT Arta Mulia is a member of the Bumi Sentosa Group.
