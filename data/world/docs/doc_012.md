# 2023 Corporate Roundup: Key Strategic Partnerships and Investments

**Jakarta, 2023** – In a continued display of strategic growth and collaborative expansion across the Indonesian corporate landscape, several major entities have solidified their market positions through significant investments and long-standing partnerships. This roundup highlights the key commercial developments that have shaped the operational frameworks of these organizations.

**PT Mitra Lestari Sejahtera** demonstrated its commitment to the startup ecosystem by investing in **PT Dharma Bahari** in the Seri A round. This strategic capital injection amounted to IDR 288 billion in 2022, marking a pivotal moment for both companies as they look to scale their respective operations.

In the realm of logistics and retail, **PT Rimba Raya** has maintained a robust distribution partnership with **PT Karya Pratama** since 2017. This enduring alliance has been instrumental in optimizing supply chain efficiency and ensuring consistent product availability for their shared customer base.

Furthermore, **Samudra Berkah** has leveraged a critical supply partnership with **PT Bintang Teguh** since 2019. This collaboration has provided **Samudra Berkah** with a reliable foundation for its procurement needs, reinforcing its operational stability over the past several years.

These developments underscore the broader trend of strategic alignment and capital deployment within the Indonesian business sector, as companies seek to enhance their competitive edge through targeted investments and durable commercial relationships.
