# Company Profile, Board Composition and Shareholder Structure

**Company Profile**
Graha Wibawa Sejahtera is a prominent entity within the Indonesian corporate landscape. The company's ownership structure is defined by three principal shareholders who have maintained their positions over several years.

**Shareholder Structure**
The capital structure of Graha Wibawa Sejahtera is held by the following individuals:
*   Samiah Andriani has held a 50% stake in Graha Wibawa Sejahtera since 2013.
*   Raden Prasasta has held a 25% stake in Graha Wibawa Sejahtera since 2016.
*   Oni Prasasta has held a 25% stake in Graha Wibawa Sejahtera since 2017.

**Board Composition**
The governance of Graha Wibawa Sejahtera is overseen by a dedicated board of directors and commissioners. The current composition includes:
*   Oni Prasasta has served as Komisaris Utama at Graha Wibawa Sejahtera since 2009.
*   Chelsea Yuniar has served as Direktur Utama at Graha Wibawa Sejahtera since 2018.
*   Lanang Halim has served as Direktur at Graha Wibawa Sejahtera since 2020.
