# Company Profile, Board Composition and Shareholder Structure

PT Sinar Mandala Investama is the principal holding entity within the group, maintaining significant control over its key operating subsidiaries. The company’s portfolio includes PT Fajar Mulia, RPFK, Harapan Abadi, and PT Rimba Kencana, all of which are classified as subsidiaries of PT Sinar Mandala Investama.

PT Fajar Mulia has been a subsidiary of PT Sinar Mandala Investama since 2019. PT Sinar Mandala Investama has held a 95% stake in PT Fajar Mulia since 2013. The board of directors and commissioners for PT Fajar Mulia includes Victoria Simanjuntak, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2008, and Hairyanto Simanjuntak, who has served as Direktur Utama since 2019.

RPFK has been a subsidiary of PT Sinar Mandala Investama since 2008. In 2020, PT Sinar Mandala Investama acquired a 78% stake in RPFK. Additionally, PT Graha Makmur Sejahtera has held a 4% stake in RPFK since 2017. The leadership team at RPFK consists of Queen Anggraini, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2017, and Nabila Nurdiyanti, who has served as Direktur Utama since 2020. The board also includes independent commissioners Yoga Mandala, who has served as Komisaris Independen since 2006, and Lanang Halim, who has served as Komisaris Independen since 2012. Virman Siregar has served as Direktur Keuangan at RPFK since 2014.

Harapan Abadi has been a subsidiary of PT Sinar Mandala Investama since 2017. PT Sinar Mandala Investama has held a 70% stake in Harapan Abadi since 2011. The governance structure for Harapan Abadi features Hairyanto Simanjuntak, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2009, and Victoria Simanjuntak, who has served as Direktur Utama since 2013.

PT Rimba Kencana has been a subsidiary of PT Sinar Mandala Investama since 2014. PT Sinar Mandala Investama has held an 87% stake in PT Rimba Kencana since 2016. The board composition for PT Rimba Kencana includes Hairyanto Simanjuntak, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2008, and Bahuraksa Simanjuntak, who has served as Direktur Utama since 2014.
