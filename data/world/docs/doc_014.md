# Strategic Alliances and Corporate Milestones in 2025

**Jakarta, 2025** – As the corporate landscape continues to evolve, key industry players are reaffirming their long-standing strategic commitments. This roundup highlights significant partnership milestones across various sectors, underscoring the enduring nature of these business relationships.

In the logistics and distribution sector, **Pelita Bahari** has maintained a robust distribution partnership with **PT Graha Pratama** since 2015. This decade-long collaboration has been instrumental in expanding market reach and optimizing supply chain efficiency for both entities.

Meanwhile, in the industrial and manufacturing space, **PT Indah Teguh Tbk** continues to leverage its joint venture with **PT Citra Nirwana**, a strategic alliance that has been active since 2012. This partnership remains a cornerstone of their operational strategy, driving innovation and shared growth over the past thirteen years.

Additionally, **BKQM** has sustained its supply partnership with **PT Arta Mulia** since 2018. This relationship has proven vital in ensuring consistent material flow and quality standards, reflecting a stable and productive cooperation that has endured for seven years.

These enduring partnerships highlight the strength of established corporate networks in Indonesia’s dynamic business environment.
