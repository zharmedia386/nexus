# Sinar Semesta Group: Corporate Structure Overview

**Observations**

Our review of the Sinar Semesta Group’s corporate structure indicates a diversified portfolio of operating entities. The group currently comprises seven distinct members, spanning both listed and unlisted entities.

The listed constituents of the Sinar Semesta Group include PT Bintang Wibawa Tbk, PT Delta Berkah Tbk, and PT Mitra Agung Tbk. These entities represent the publicly traded segments of the group's operations.

In addition to the listed companies, the Sinar Semesta Group includes four unlisted members: PT Gemilang Makmur, PT Dharma Kencana, PT Tunas Mandala, and PT Mitra Wibawa. These entities operate within the group structure without a public listing.

This composition highlights the group's reliance on a mix of public market access through its Tbk entities and private operational units to support its broader business objectives.
