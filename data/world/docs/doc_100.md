# Arta Utama Group: Structural Overview

**Observations**

We note that the Arta Utama Group comprises a diversified portfolio of entities. Based on our review of the group structure, the following members are identified within the conglomerate:

*   DKSP
*   Rimba Mulia
*   PT Mitra Bahari
*   PT Bintang Kreasi Tbk
*   Lintas Lancar
*   PT Tirta Kencana
*   Samudra Sukses

These entities collectively form the constituent parts of the Arta Utama Group as observed in 2022.
