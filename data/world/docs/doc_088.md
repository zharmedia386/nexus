# Group Structure: Observations on the Composition of Grup Sinar Mandala

**Observations**

Our review of the corporate structure identifies seven distinct entities that constitute the current membership of the Grup Sinar Mandala. The group's composition includes a mix of publicly listed and private entities, reflecting a diversified operational footprint.

Specifically, the following entities are members of the Grup Sinar Mandala:

*   PT Nusantara Gemilang Tbk
*   Lintas Makmur
*   PT Fajar Mulia
*   PT Harapan Abadi
*   PT Rimba Perkasa Tbk
*   PT Rimba Kencana
*   Tirta Mulia

This structure indicates that the Grup Sinar Mandala maintains a broad base of affiliated companies, with both listed (Tbk) and unlisted entities contributing to the group's overall profile.
