# Public Disclosure Notice: Update on Board of Commissioners and Directors

**Subject: Changes in Board Composition at ITNV, PT Gemilang Sukses Tbk, and BKQM**

This notice provides a factual update regarding the service periods of specific individuals within the board of commissioners and directors of ITNV, PT Gemilang Sukses Tbk, and BKQM.

**ITNV**
Ulya Anggraini served as Komisaris Independen at ITNV from 2014 until 2025. Oliva Hariyah serves as Komisaris Independen at ITNV since 2025.

**PT Gemilang Sukses Tbk**
Respati Setiawan served as Direktur Keuangan at PT Gemilang Sukses Tbk from 2012 until 2014.

**BKQM**
Ifa Prastuti served as Direktur Operasional at BKQM from 2010 until 2017.
