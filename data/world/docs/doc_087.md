# Grup Sinar Mandala: Group Structure Overview

This note provides an overview of the corporate structure within the Grup Sinar Mandala. Our observations indicate that the group comprises several distinct entities. Specifically, Sinar Mandala Investama is a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala. Additionally, PT Graha Makmur Sejahtera is a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala. The group also includes PT Cahaya Mulia Tbk, which is a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala. Furthermore, PT Mulia Sukses is a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala. PT Fajar Kreasi is also a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala. We note that PT Indah Wibawa is a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala. Finally, PT Gemilang Mandala is a member of the Grup Sinar Mandala.
