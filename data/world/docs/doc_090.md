# Mega Sentosa Group: Structural Overview

We observe that the Mega Sentosa Group comprises a diverse portfolio of entities. The group membership includes Citra Harmoni, PT Sinar Mulia, PT Tunas Unggul Tbk, SATR, Bintang Pratama, PT Prima Makmur Tbk, and PT Cahaya Kencana. These seven entities collectively form the current structural composition of the group as of 2020.
