# Group Structure: Observations on the Composition of Grup Sinar Semesta

**Observations**

We note that the corporate ecosystem of Grup Sinar Semesta comprises a diverse set of entities. Our review of the group structure identifies the following members:

*   Sinar Semesta Capital is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.
*   Mitra Unggul Capital is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.
*   Citra Cemerlang is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.
*   PT Wahana Sakti Tbk is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.
*   TLFY is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.
*   PT Harapan Teguh is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.
*   PT Fajar Makmur is a member of the Grup Sinar Semesta.

These entities collectively form the constituent parts of the Grup Sinar Semesta as of 2025.
