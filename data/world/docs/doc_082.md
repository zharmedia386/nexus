# Corporate Governance Update: PT Tunas Gemilang and PT Karya Pratama Leadership Structures

**JAKARTA** – A review of the current corporate structures for PT Tunas Gemilang and PT Karya Pratama highlights the long-standing tenure of key executives and the significant equity positions held by their respective founders.

PT Tunas Gemilang was founded by Virman Saragih in 1996. Since 2018, Virman Saragih has held a 25% stake in the company. The board and management of PT Tunas Gemilang have remained stable, with Lala Winarsih serving as Komisaris Utama since 2006. Daruna Mandala has served as Direktur Utama since 2010, while Prasetya Pranowo has held the position of Direktur since 2005.

In a similar vein, PT Karya Pratama was established by Dina Mardhiyah in 2001. Dina Mardhiyah has maintained a 36% stake in PT Karya Pratama since 2008. The company’s leadership team includes Wadi Budiman, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2014. Queen Anggraini has been the Direktur Utama since 2006, and Daruna Mandala has served as Direktur at PT Karya Pratama since 2015.
