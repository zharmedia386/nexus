# Corporate Governance Update: PT Delta Utama Tbk and Tunas Kreasi Leadership and Ownership Structures

**JAKARTA** – In a roundup of corporate governance disclosures for 2025, key leadership and ownership details have been confirmed for PT Delta Utama Tbk and Tunas Kreasi.

PT Delta Utama Tbk, a subsidiary of PT Buana Agung (Persero) since 2017, continues to be led by a long-standing management team. Margana Tarihoran has served as Direktur Utama at PT Delta Utama Tbk since 2013. The company’s financial leadership is anchored by Eka Permadi, who has held the position of Direktur Keuangan at PT Delta Utama Tbk since 2007.

The supervisory board of PT Delta Utama Tbk includes Wahyu Iswahyudi, who has served as Komisaris Utama since 2014, and Cakrawala Maulana, who has held the role of Komisaris Independen since 2016. PT Buana Agung (Persero) has maintained a 56% stake in PT Delta Utama Tbk since 2006.

In a separate corporate matter, Tunas Kreasi, which was founded by Teddy Sitompul in 2001, has maintained a consistent leadership structure. Karna Iswahyudi has served as Direktur Utama at Tunas Kreasi since 2011, while Oliva Hariyah has held the position of Komisaris Utama since 2009. Teddy Sitompul has held a 21% stake in Tunas Kreasi since 2016.
