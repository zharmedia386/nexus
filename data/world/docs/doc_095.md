# Group Structure: Mulia Lancar Group

The Mulia Lancar Group comprises a diverse portfolio of entities spanning various sectors. The group structure includes the following members:

*   PT Mulia Lancar Capital
*   PT Graha Wibawa Sejahtera
*   PT Karya Cemerlang
*   PT Fajar Berkah Tbk
*   PT Bumi Kencana Tbk
*   Karya Semesta
*   PT Prima Lestari Tbk

These entities collectively form the operational and financial backbone of the Mulia Lancar Group.
