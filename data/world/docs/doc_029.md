# Company Profile, Board Composition and Shareholder Structure

Karya Abadi operates as a subsidiary of PT Pelita Gemilang Capital, a relationship established in 2015. The company's shareholder structure is anchored by PT Pelita Gemilang Capital, which has held a 66% stake in Karya Abadi since 2019. Additionally, PT Nusantara Kreasi Sejahtera has maintained a 4% stake in Karya Abadi since 2006.

The board of directors and commissioners at Karya Abadi comprises the following individuals:

*   **Jaswadi Maulana** serves as Direktur Utama at Karya Abadi, a position he has held since 2008.
*   **Dalimin Uwais** serves as Direktur Keuangan at Karya Abadi, a position he has held since 2011.
*   **Kartika Pertiwi** serves as Komisaris Utama at Karya Abadi, a position she has held since 2008.
*   **Lala Winarsih** serves as Komisaris Independen at Karya Abadi, a position she has held since 2005.
*   **Galang Waskita** serves as Komisaris Independen at Karya Abadi, a position he has held since 2017.
