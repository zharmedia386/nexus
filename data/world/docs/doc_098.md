# Nusantara Sakti Group: Structural Overview

We observe that the Nusantara Sakti Group comprises a diverse portfolio of entities. PT Tunas Teguh is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group. PT Citra Kencana is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group. PT Cahaya Teguh is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group. JKEX is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group. PT Graha Pratama is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group. Citra Lancar is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group. PT Gemilang Sukses Tbk is a member of the Nusantara Sakti Group.
