# Corporate Governance Update: BKQM and Citra Nirwana Leadership Structures

**JAKARTA** – In a review of corporate governance structures for selected Indonesian entities, the board compositions of BKQM and Citra Nirwana have been confirmed as of 2022.

BKQM, which has been a subsidiary of Buana Agung (Persero) since 2016, maintains its leadership team with no recent changes noted in the current period. Buana Agung (Persero) has held a 65% stake in BKQM since 2006. The company’s executive and supervisory boards remain stable, with Prasetya Utama serving as Direktur Utama since 2010 and Daruna Mandala serving as Direktur Keuangan since 2014. Taufik Mangunsong has served as Direktur Operasional since 2017. On the supervisory side, Drajat Januar has served as Komisaris Utama since 2020, while Lantar Natsir has served as Komisaris Independen since 2010.

Separately, Citra Nirwana, founded by Lasmanto Sitompul in 1998, continues to operate under its established leadership. Lasmanto Sitompul has held a 39% stake in the company since 2013. The company’s management includes Ismail Sihotang, who has served as Direktur Utama since 2017. Ulya Anggraini has served as Komisaris Utama at Citra Nirwana since 2010.
