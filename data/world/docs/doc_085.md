# Group Structure: Observations on the Composition of the Grup Pelita Gemilang

**Observations**

The Grup Pelita Gemilang comprises a diverse set of entities operating within the Indonesian corporate landscape. Based on our review of the group structure, we identify the following members:

*   Pelita Gemilang Capital is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
*   PT Nusantara Kreasi Sejahtera is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
*   PT Karya Abadi Tbk is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
*   PT Karya Lancar is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
*   Sinar Berkah is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
*   PT Buana Raya Tbk is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
*   Arta Semesta is a member of the Grup Pelita Gemilang.
